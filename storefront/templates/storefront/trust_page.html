{% if page == 'privacy' %}

IKart only collects the information needed to process orders, provide support, and improve the store. We never sell personal information.

Payment details are handled by the configured payment provider and are not stored in this application.

Lux, our shopping assistant, uses an AI service provided by Groq to write its replies. When you chat with Lux, your message is sent to Groq, along with your name if you are signed in and, when you ask about your orders, a summary of your recent orders (order numbers, dates, items, totals, payment and delivery status). Your address, phone number, email and payment details are never sent. IKart does not store your chat messages; please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in the chat.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item passes inspection.
